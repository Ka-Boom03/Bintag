Google OAuth and API Services Data Usage

Bintag uses Google OAuth to authenticate users and provide core functionality. Bintag's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Data We Request from Google

When you sign in using Google, we request the following specific OAuth scopes:

  • Email Address (email scope): We collect your primary Google email address to serve as your unique account identifier and to send essential service notifications.
  • Basic Profile Information (profile scope): We collect your name and profile picture to personalize your user dashboard and display your identity within the application.
  • OpenID Connect (openid scope): We use this to securely authenticate your login session without requiring you to create a separate password.

2. How We Use Google Data

We use the data obtained through Google APIs exclusively to provide the Bintag Service. Specifically, your data is used to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage your Bintag account.
  • Link your scanned inventory and QR codes to your specific user profile.

Prohibited Uses: We will never use your Google user data for advertising purposes, we will not use it to build behavioral profiles, and we will not feed your data into generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google user data. We only share this data with third-party Service Providers strictly necessary to operate the Service (e.g., secure cloud hosting providers like Google Cloud and Cloudflare). All such Service Providers are contractually bound to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data and are prohibited from using it for any other purpose.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your Google data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We retain your Google-provided email and profile information only for as long as your Bintag account is active.

  • User-Initiated Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated Google data at any time by navigating to your Account Settings and clicking "Delete Account." Upon request, your data is permanently purged from our active databases within 7 days.
  • Inactivity: If your account remains inactive for 24 consecutive months, we will automatically delete your account and all associated Google data.
